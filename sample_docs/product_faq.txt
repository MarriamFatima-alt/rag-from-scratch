Acme Robotics — Product FAQ

What is the battery life of the Acme Home Robot?
The Acme Home Robot runs for approximately 6 hours on a full charge under normal use, and 3.5 hours under heavy continuous cleaning mode. A full charge from empty takes about 2 hours on the included dock.

Is the robot compatible with voice assistants?
Yes. The robot supports both Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant. Apple HomeKit support is planned for a future firmware update but is not currently available.

What is covered under warranty?
All Acme Home Robots come with a 2-year limited warranty covering manufacturing defects and battery degradation below 70% capacity. Physical damage from drops or water exposure is not covered.

How do I reset the robot to factory settings?
Hold the power button and the home button together for 10 seconds until the status light flashes blue three times. This will erase all saved maps and schedules.

Can I use the robot in a multi-story home?
Yes, the robot can store maps for up to 5 different floors. You switch between saved floor maps from the mobile app before starting a cleaning cycle.

What should I do if the robot gets stuck?
The robot will send a push notification and pause its cycle if it detects it is stuck for more than 2 minutes. It will attempt to return to its dock automatically if the battery drops below 15%.
